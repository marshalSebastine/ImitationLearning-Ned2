# Recording rules for round 2 (to try on Saturday 14.11.26)

For Emranul, who records every episode himself (Emranul, 14.11.26; Marshal records none). One page. One person and
one set of rules, so that the robot learns one way of pushing. These rules are a first version: the first eight
episodes on Saturday (T01 to T04, two each) show which rule is hard to keep, and then the rules are changed, not you.

Why these rules. On 11.11 and 12.11 the robot stopped 1 to 2 cm beside the end of the line and drifted to the edge
of the line in bends [measured with the ruler on 12.11.26 by Emranul; seen in the camera pictures]. It copies what
the recordings show: in round 1 the puck was within 7 mm of the middle of the line in 68 % of the pictures, and the
end of the line was covered in 52 to 60 % of the episodes [measured in the check of 11.11.26].

## Before each sheet

1. Lay the sheet as the picture on the recording page shows: the round start of the line (end A) on the tape mark,
   the first straight piece along the taped line. Tape the sheet at its corners, away from the line.
2. The sheet stays taped until all its episodes are done. The page offers them one after the other.
3. Put the puck on the round start of the line, in the middle.

## While you guide the arm (motors off)

4. **Middle of the line.** Keep the middle of the puck on the middle of the line the whole way. The black line shows
   equally on both sides of the puck.
5. **Push from behind,** slowly and at a steady speed. The block stays behind the puck and touches it.
6. **Sharp bend.** Go round the outside of the bend with the block while the forearm turns. Do not turn on the spot,
   unless the bend is too tight for anything else.
7. **Loop.** Walk the block round the puck the same way every time, as you did in round 1.
8. **Crossing.** Straight across. **Dashed line.** Exactly like a solid line: do not slow down or stop at a gap.
9. **The end.** Be on the middle of the line 3 cm before the end. Then push straight on until the puck covers the
   round end of the line completely, and stop there. Never stop in the middle of a line.
10. **Wrist.** Keep the wrist as it is at the start. Do not bend it on the way.

## In the replay (the robot repeats your path)

11. Watch the puck. If it leaves the line: pause, press `h`, guide it back to the middle of the line and carry on to
    the end. Record the episode again only when the puck was lost completely.

## The score

12. Score 3 only when the puck stayed in the middle all the way and covers the end. Score 2 when it wobbled but
    stayed on the line. Score 1 for anything else. Be strict: a strict score helps more than a kind one.

## After the first eight episodes

Each of you says, for every rule: easy, hard, or not possible. Write it down. Rules 6, 7 and 10 are the ones most
likely to change.

Not yet tried by anyone: these rules as a whole. Rules 4 and 9 were tried by Emranul alone on 12.11.26 only in talk,
not in recordings.
