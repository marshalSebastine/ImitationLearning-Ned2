# Proofreading of version 2 of the plan: complete results

Two independent readers proofread version 2 of `2026-11-11_plan_after_first_robot_test.md` on the night of 11 to
12.11.26. Every correction is given with the exact old and new text. Their scripts and outputs are in
`proofread_fidelity/` and `proofread_sitting_a/` next to this file. Nothing was run on the robot.

## Is version 2 faithful to its sources and consistent with itself? (`proofread_fidelity`)

**Verdict.** Version 2 is faithful in its facts but not yet fit to act on as written. Sections 1 to 4 and 9 match the two source files except for one mixed count (the stop per sheet), one baseline that belongs to another number (0.79 to 0.82) and a few small slips; all eight reported exam rules are carried through and no 'if printing is allowed' is left [searched]; my own calculation shows that the 42 cm of decision 2 can be met by the six sheets. The faults lie in how the four checkers' fixes were merged. The three that matter most: (1) 'the chosen step', on which all of sitting B and the driver decision rest, is undefined when A4's rule takes no step on, and the checker's own figures make that the likely outcome (F01); (2) the tools before sitting A are booked at one to two days against a calculated three to six, and the three sittings at 7 hours against about 9 to 10, so the first two weeks of the calendar do not hold (F02, F11); (3) two checked fixes that sitting A cannot run without are missing: how the sheet gets onto its recorded place, and the safety steps for the arm's new start move (F03, F04). After these: A3's word 'finished' would have failed the control run of 11.11 itself (F05), the stall rule contradicts the rule for ending runs by hand (F06), 'lost' has no number although the driver decision counts it (F07), decision 2 does not say where the mark is or how a provided sheet lies (F08), and the message to the professor is back in fifth place although the driver is decided before 23.11 (F09). Where the checkers disagreed, version 2's choices are defensible: the path stall rule (the only one tested at the pace of short steps), the robot-session checker's count rule for A4 (at most 1 in 18 by luck; its measure gives fail, fail, pass on the logs), the evidence checker's sheet-04 block with a rule and its decision rule after sitting B, and 3 cm past the end as the limit. What the merge lost each time is the sentence that covered the other case, and no fix that was set aside carries a stated reason (F24 lists the smaller ones). Nothing was connected to the robot, no git change was made, nothing was trained, no existing file was edited; my two scripts and their outputs are in D:/AI_Project/.claude/worktrees/training-analysis/06_deliverables/research_notes/Model and training/evidence/robot_test_2026-11-11/plan_review/proofread_fidelity/ .
