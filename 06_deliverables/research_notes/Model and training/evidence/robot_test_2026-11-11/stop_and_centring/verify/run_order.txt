Verifier's folder for the analyst "stop_and_centring" (11.11.26). Everything here was written by the verifier, apart
from the analyst's own code, to recompute the claims that matter for a decision. Nothing connects to the robot;
pictures, logged joints, frames.csv and saved model answers are only read; every result is written into this folder.

Python: D:/AI_Project/.venv/Scripts/python.exe, with this folder as the working directory (each script a few seconds
to two minutes).

My own tools (not the analyst's):
  my_measure.py       red top face of the puck -> foot on the table (extent in 16 directions), dark-on-paper line mask,
                      table <-> picture through the project's forearm camera, the camera with a shift and a roll

  centring_tools.py   the sideways offset with puck and line from the SAME picture
  contact_sheet.py    raw pictures side by side, to be looked at
