## How to read this file

- **Where numbers come from.** Every number below comes from a script an agent ran or a file an agent read; the agent's note names it. The few numbers added in this synthesis come from three scripts in F/synthesis/, each with its printout next to it: synthesis_verdicts.py (verdict counts; per-finding table verdict_table.csv), synthesis_cleaning.py (how the proposed cleaning rules overlap on frames.csv and thumbnails.npy) and synthesis_named_pictures.py (where the named hand pictures sit in their episodes).
- **Markers.** [measured] = computed from the data. [calculated] = derived from measured numbers with stated assumptions. [guess] = judgement, not tested.
- **Final verdict of each finding.**
  - *confirmed*: both checkers confirmed it.
  - *corrected*: at least one checker changed a number, the wording or the conclusion, and nobody refuted it. The numbers given are the final ones; the text says which checker changed what. Many corrected findings keep their headline.
  - *refuted*: a checker showed by measurement that the core claim is wrong. Refuted findings are dropped and listed at the end of their section, together with refuted parts of corrected findings.
  - *unverifiable*: the core claim is a guess about a cause that neither checker could test with the data, whatever label they gave it.
- Where the auditor and a checker differ only by rounding or by a slightly different definition, both values are given as a range (for example "3,009 to 3,012").
- "05-16" means the episode of plan 05-16 (try 1 unless a try is named); "frame 065" or "f065" is the picture frames/065.jpg of that episode; "frame_index 10470" is the row number in frames.csv.

**The six messages that run through all dimensions** (details in the sections):

1. The labels are aligned correctly and follow the labelling rule. Their noise has three sources: a continuous, wandering hand-guided move is cut into 8 uneven bins, the forearm twist that happens on most steps is not in the label, and at large forearm turns the label frame is off by several degrees.
2. 449 rows (row 0 of every episode and the first row after every hand correction) are null moves; their pictures sometimes show hands. 51 more are duplicated final points whose picture is almost the stop picture.
3. The end labels are wrong for 30 episodes (start_end) and 11 (plan_end); several start kinds do not describe how the sheet was really laid.
4. Pictures of one sheet are near-twins within a recording block. Only leave-one-sheet-out tests unseen tracks; episode splits are optimistic even for the known sheets.
5. Score 1 differs little from score 2; dropping it thins the hardest starts.
6. Sheet, day, light, operator and camera period are tied together, so no split separates them.

## 2. Label noise and consistency (label_consistency)

Notes: F/label_consistency/note.md; checkers F/label_consistency/check_recompute/note.md, F/label_consistency/check_challenge/note.md.

### Findings that survived

**label_consistency-1. The labels are aligned: row i's label is the move from picture i to picture i+1.** [measured] Confirmed by both. Row i's logged move against the arm-model tip move from row i to i+1: median 0.092 mm (p95 0.272, max 1.068 to 1.071) over 18,312 pairs; against the off-by-one alternative 5.46 mm. Forearm change against the joint-4 difference: median 0.089 deg (off by one: 1.844). A label recomputed from the executed move equals the logged action in 18,133 to 18,138 of 18,312 rows (99.0 %; off by one 44.9 %). The time order capture, decision, move return, next capture holds in 18,312 of 18,312 pairs. The challenge checker confirmed it from the pictures alone: the content shift from picture i to i+1 correlates with move i at r 0.914 (sideways) and 0.845 (forward), but only 0.69 with move i-1 and 0.60 to 0.62 with move i+1. Pictures captured within 0.06 s of the move's end show a slightly smaller shift (0.93 of the expected size, 187 pairs); a few may have been taken before the camera settled (guess). Train with picture i and label i as stored.

**label_consistency-2. The rule is applied exactly; execution error flips about 1 % of labels, and two other noise sources are as large or larger.** [measured; one estimate calculated] Corrected by the challenge checker. The rule matches 18,517 of 18,522 rows (the 5 misses sit on thresholds after rounding). Labels that flip between the commanded and the executed move: 175 to 179 of 18,312 (1.0 %); executed minus commanded angle: median 0.24 deg, p95 1.44 to 1.45, p99 2.66 to 2.80. The challenge checker found two more sources:
- *Label-frame error at large forearm turns.* The picture content moves at an angle to the label direction: a median +8.64 deg at turns of -80 deg or less (454 pairs), +3.37 at -80 to -60, and -3.28 above +80 (730 pairs), against +0.56 near a straight forearm (1,502 pairs), and the same in all four picture regions. 3,202 of 18,522 rows (17.3 %) are turned beyond 80 deg and 7,275 (39.3 %) beyond 57 deg, the turn up to which config.py says the heading model was checked. Turning those labels by the measured medians would change 403 of 16,034 moving labels (2.5 %, calculated), more than execution error does. The cause (the heading model beyond 57 deg) is a guess.
- *The puck put back by hand.* After a hand correction the puck sat more than 3 px of 160 (about 5 mm, calculated) from its reference position in 24 of 179 measurable corrections (13.4 %), and more than 5 px in 7 (3.9 %); 14 of the 24 are on sheet 04. The replayed correction labels then steer for a puck that sits elsewhere.

**label_consistency-3. About a fifth of the moves lie within 5 deg of a bin edge.** [measured] Corrected by the challenge checker. ### 11.2 Decisions the data forces

**Splits and evaluation**

1. **Never split pictures at random**: 81 % of validation pictures would have a training twin. [duplicates_leakage-6]
2. **Leave one sheet out (6 folds) is the main test of unseen tracks**, each fold reported against its own trivial baselines, because they differ widely: always-forward on moving rows 68.4, 43.1, 38.9, 38.8, 42.9 and 31.1 % with median angle errors of 13.6, 37.6, 42.4, 47.3, 41.9 and 65.3 deg on sheets 01 to 06; repeat-previous-move 52.1 % on average; a picture-free sheet, end and progress baseline 40.0 % and 36.4 deg. A six-fold mean is uncertain (look-up agreement 0.280 ± 0.060) and mixes track difficulty with generalisation. Each fold also holds out a day, a light, an operator mix, a camera period and some start kinds (table 5.3). [duplicates_leakage-7, -8, -15; notes_context-12]
3. **"Known sheet, new session" (half of the exam) needs leave-one-block-out inside sheets 02 and 04**: twins are bound to recording blocks (0.26 to 0.55 within a block, 0.000 to 0.003 across blocks of the same sheet), so an episode split is an upper bound even for the known sheets. [duplicates_leakage-3, -15]
4. **Choose epochs and settings on sheet or block folds**, not on held-out episodes of the training sheets, and do not use the calendar day as a fold unit (the sittings cross midnight). [duplicates_leakage-15]
5. **Group every statistic by episode or larger units**: consecutive rows are strongly dependent (about 1,100 to 1,700 independent views of picture content and about 3,100 independent labels among 18,522 rows). [duplicates_leakage-7, label_consistency-5]
6. **Judge models by angle error and closed-loop runs**, with balanced accuracy or macro F1 per sheet for any class head; 8-way accuracy hardly separates models (always-forward 40.3 %, picture-free 39.9 to 40.4 %, a leaky look-up 42.3 %). Report start rows separately. [duplicates_leakage-6, label_consistency-6]
7. **One evaluation goal**: the puck centre within a stated distance of the line end, not is_at_goal (the tip's distance to the demonstrated stop); record wall-clock time per trial. [notes_context-13, -23]
8. Fit normalisation and class weights on the training folds only; train the exam model on all six sheets once the settings are chosen. [duplicates_leakage-15]

**Targets**

9. **Predict the exact move and a continuous twist, not only 8 names.** Regress (tip_forward_m, tip_left_m) and forearm_change_deg (roughly 0 to 10 deg per step): the names keep 81 to 85 % of the tip-move variance but 16.3 % of the twist; executing a class at its nominal angle is 11.95 deg off on average; 18.8 % of the moves lie within 5 deg of a bin edge; 56.1 % of the non-stop rows twist by 3 deg or more, while the turn labels catch 13. If classes are kept, keep the current 8 bins and use soft targets about 12 deg wide; the achievable class accuracy lies well below 100 % (59 to 77 % for near-identical pictures). [motion_statistics-13, class_balance-5, label_consistency-3, -6, -17]
10. **The wrist needs an output or a rule.** Joint 5 is more than 5 deg from its episode-start value in 28.0 % of rows and spans 10 deg or more in 89 of 210 episodes; holding it fixed shifts the picture centre by more than 20 px in 23.3 % of rows. [code_actions-3, -4; motion_statistics-7]
11. **hand_change is not a target** (37 steps of 3 deg or more), but the hand angle differs per start kind and is visible: a shortcut risk. [motion_statistics-8]
12. **Step length is set by the waypoint sampler** (twisting steps about half as long; guided sampling at about 9 Hz): if it is predicted, clip it at about 20 mm and know that it copies recording rules; otherwise fix the step. (Which one is open.) [motion_statistics-3, -14; code_actions-5]
13. **Stop is rare and its cue is the paper edge.** 259 stop rows after the relabelling (1.43 % of the 18,053 kept rows; synthesis_cleaning.py): weight it or train it with a tolerance window, not from is_at_goal alone (124 at-goal rows in 30 episodes come before the tip leaves the 2-cm circle again; 111 of 808 are more than 10 rows before the stop). Every line end lies 2.35 to 8.03 cm from a paper edge and the stop pictures show no line ahead, so test a printed track whose end lies away from an edge. [class_balance-11, notes_context-4, view_and_tracks-2]
14. **Decide on the label frame at large forearm turns before training on picture-frame targets**: the picture content moves +8.6 deg (turns of -80 deg or less) or -3.3 deg (above +80 deg) off the label direction; correcting it would change 403 of 16,034 moving labels (2.5 %); 39.3 % of the rows lie beyond the 57 deg up to which the heading model was checked. [label_consistency-2, -14; code_actions-16]
15. **If small_motion rows are relabelled with the next move**, the inherited move was measured in a later picture's frame (16 % change class in the row's own frame): execute the twist first or re-express the move. [class_balance further facts]
16. **No previous-action input** until the professor allows it and it is tested: repeat-previous-move alone scores 52.1 %, so it would inflate offline scores. [notes_context-20]

**Class handling and weighting**

17. **Class weights or balanced sampling** (forward 40.3 % of moving rows, back 3.4 %); the probes' square-root weights (0.59 to 2.88) are a starting point. Per-class results on fold 01 rest on 10 to 24 rows per back class. [class_balance-1, -13]
