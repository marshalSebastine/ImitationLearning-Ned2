**Subject:** Ned2 Vision Set: dimensions of the camera holder and its mounting screws (university project)

---

Dear Niryo support team,

I am a master's student at OTH Amberg-Weiden in Germany working with a Niryo Ned2 and the Vision Set in a university
project supervised by Prof. Nierhoff. Our task requires the wrist camera to look at the workspace from a slightly different
position, so our university's Maker Space will 3D-print a small bracket for us. The bracket will use the two existing screw
holes of the camera holder in the wrist and will carry the original black camera holder unchanged, so nothing is drilled or
modified on the robot.

To design the bracket correctly we need a few dimensions that we could not find in the Ned2 documentation, the NED2 STEP
file or the ned_ros repository (the STEP shows the wrist as one solid, and the Ned2 camera holder is not in the published
files). Could you please tell us, or send a drawing or 3D file for:

1. The camera holder of the Ned2 Vision Set (the black printed part with two ears): a STEP or STL file, or its outer
   dimensions and the centre distance and diameter of the two ear holes.
2. The two screws that fix the holder to the aluminium brace in the wrist: thread (for example M3) and length.
3. The position of those two threaded holes in the wrist relative to the joint-6 (hand) axis and the hand face.
4. The outer dimensions of the camera module in its housing (width, height, depth including the lens and the focus ring).
5. If available: the horizontal and vertical field of view of the camera at 640 x 480, since we found only the intrinsics
   in cam_intrinsics.yaml.

Any of these would save us a measuring session on the robot and reduce the risk of a wrong print. We are of course aware that
the bracket is our own modification and not covered by Niryo.

Robot: Ned2, software version 6.1.2. Vision Set with the ELP camera module as supplied.

Thank you very much for your help.

Kind regards,
Emranul Hoque
Team M, AI Project (Project 6), OTH Amberg-Weiden
