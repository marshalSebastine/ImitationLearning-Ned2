- Measured: in the failed runs the rod tip was on the line until 43 cm from the base, then went to 48 cm. Only 4.9 % of

- Checked and dropped as causes: laptop and arm in the camera's view; the wrist held fixed by the driver.

**Why:** Emranul pointed out that the sheet's orientation changes the result and that the exam leaves no time to try
lay-outs. The exam lets the team lay every sheet ([[project-exam-rules-reported]]).

**How to apply:** propose the lay-out rule (start cap on the mark, turn the sheet until the whole line is as near the
robot as possible, nothing farther than about 43 cm from the base centre; a tape or string on the table as the aid)
and test it before relying on it: the 43 cm is calculated, not tried. Decide the six course sheets' lay-outs on a card
beforehand. Do not read where the puck stopped from a run log: the log knows the rod tip and the planned sheet place,
not the puck (a wrong "5 cm past the end" claim on 14.11.26 was corrected by Emranul's look). Emranul's own idea of
laying the sheet landscape was right; see [[project-final-model-search]] for the training side ("far_reach_3").
